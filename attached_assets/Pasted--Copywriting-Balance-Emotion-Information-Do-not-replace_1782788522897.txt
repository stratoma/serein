# Copywriting Balance: Emotion + Information

Do not replace informative product copy with poetic copy.

SEREIN is an editorial luxury brand, but it is also a handcrafted skincare company. Customers should leave inspired by the brand and confident in the formulation.

Every revision should preserve both emotional resonance and practical clarity.

## The Rule

Layer poetry onto information.

Never replace information with poetry.

The copy should always answer two questions:

1. How does this make me feel?
2. Why does this work?

The answer to both should exist together.

---

# Use Emotional Copy For

- Homepage hero
- Editorial sections
- Campaign pages
- Collection introductions
- Lifestyle storytelling
- Brand philosophy
- About page

This is where language such as:

- Luxury for living
- The study of warmth
- Evocative texture
- Quiet richness
- The quiet architecture of everyday ritual

belongs.

This copy should create atmosphere and emotion.

---

# Use Informative Copy For

- Ingredient descriptions
- Product benefits
- Product pages
- FAQs
- How to Use
- Formulation details

Preserve clear educational information including:

- fatty acids
- vitamins
- antioxidants
- skin barrier support
- hydration
- nourishment
- texture
- absorption
- botanical properties

Customers should understand exactly why each ingredient benefits the skin.

---

# Preferred Writing Structure

Whenever possible, follow this sequence:

1. Scientific or functional benefit
2. Sensory experience
3. Emotional feeling

Example:

**Instead of:**

"Drawn from the seed of the mango fruit. It melts on contact, leaving skin velvety and settled."

Prefer:

"Rich in oleic and stearic fatty acids, mango butter deeply nourishes the skin while restoring softness and resilience. Drawn from the seed of the mango fruit, it melts on contact, leaving skin velvety, comforted, and beautifully cared for."

Another example:

Instead of removing Vitamin E's benefits, combine them:

"Vitamin E is a powerful antioxidant that helps protect the skin from environmental stress while supporting the skin's moisture barrier. Quiet in its work, it preserves what the skin already holds."

---

# Homepage Copy

The homepage should balance inspiration with clarity.

Do not make every sentence abstract.

Visitors should immediately understand what SEREIN offers.

For example:

Headline:
"The quiet architecture of everyday ritual."

Supporting copy:
"Small-batch body butters crafted for everyday ritual, made with rich botanical butters that deeply nourish and soften the skin."

Likewise, retain clear benefit-driven language in feature cards.

Preferred:

Deep nourishment.

Supporting line:
"Skin that knows it has been tended to."

Rather than replacing the benefit entirely.

---

# Brand Principle

Emotion attracts.

Information builds trust.

Together they create confidence.

Every page should leave visitors feeling both emotionally connected to SEREIN and confident in the quality and effectiveness of its formulations.

Never sacrifice clarity for poetry.

Never sacrifice beauty for technical language.

The strongest SEREIN copy is elegant, intelligent, sensory, and informative all at once.
