# SEREIN WEBSITE BUILD SPECIFICATION

## Version 2.0 — Market Ready | Customer Focused

### Brand Statement

SEREIN creates handcrafted body care inspired by quiet rituals, natural materials, and the beauty of becoming.

The website should feel calm, tactile, and intentional—not sparse or difficult to navigate.

Every page should answer the customer's questions while preserving the brand's atmosphere.
