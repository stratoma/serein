# SEREIN Website Image Integration Instructions

Incorporate all uploaded SEREIN imagery into the website with a refined editorial structure.

The visual world should follow the SEREIN brand philosophy:

SEREIN is the study of warmth.
Through skin, light, texture, and ritual, we reveal the quiet richness that already exists.

The site should feel like luxury for living, not luxury for display. Use imagery to create warmth, rhythm, intimacy, and evocative texture.

## Image Types to Integrate

I will upload:

- Scent cards
- Ingredient graphics
- Scent note graphics
- Editorial imagery with text
- Product stills
- Lifestyle and ritual imagery
- Website hero or campaign graphics

Use these assets throughout the website as designed brand elements, not random decorations.

## Creative Direction

The imagery should feel:

- Warm
- Editorial
- Spacious
- Grounded
- Cinematic
- Tactile
- Ritual-led
- Quietly luxurious

Prioritize amber light, natural shadow, textured materials, Black skin in luminous light, stone, linen, ceramics, botanicals, amber glass, wood, and earth tones.

Do not over-brighten or flatten images. Preserve warmth, shadow, grain, and dimension.

## Homepage Integration

Use the strongest editorial hero image at the top of the homepage.

Hero requirements:

- Full-width or cinematic split layout
- Text overlay only when readable
- Clear call-to-action
- Mobile crop must preserve the product, face, or core focal point
- Maintain generous spacing
- Avoid clutter

Below the hero, create visual rhythm using:

- Scent cards as featured collection tiles
- Ingredient graphics as educational sections
- Editorial text imagery as atmospheric brand storytelling
- Product stills for shop sections
- Lifestyle imagery for ritual and brand philosophy sections

The homepage should feel like a guided editorial experience, not a standard product grid.

## Product Pages

For each product page, integrate:

1. Product still
2. Scent card
3. Ingredient graphic
4. Scent notes graphic
5. Editorial/lifestyle image
6. Ritual image where available

Suggested section flow:

- Product hero
- Scent story
- Texture and ritual
- Key ingredients
- Scent notes
- How to use
- Related products

Use uploaded scent cards and ingredient graphics as premium visual education assets.

## Collections Page

Use editorial imagery to introduce each scent world:

- Transform
- Root
- Become

Each scent should feel like its own atmosphere while remaining part of the SEREIN system.

Use image-led cards with restrained copy and clear shop links.

## Image Handling

Optimize all uploaded images for web performance.

Create responsive versions for:

- Desktop
- Tablet
- Mobile

Use lazy loading below the fold.

Use descriptive alt text that reflects the image and brand language.

Example alt text:

“Amber glass jar of SEREIN body butter on warm stone with botanical shadows.”

Do not use generic alt text like “image” or “product photo.”

## Layout Guidance

Use generous white space.

Let images breathe.

Avoid overcrowded sections.

Mix full-bleed imagery with quiet framed image blocks.

Use asymmetry where appropriate so the site feels composed, not overly staged.

The visual rhythm should feel like:

light
texture
ritual
product
pause
story
product

## Brand Copy Alignment

When placing or revising copy around imagery, preserve this voice:

Quiet.
Warm.
Refined.
Sensory.
Grounded.
Intentional.

Use phrases such as:

- Luxury for living
- Evocative texture
- The study of warmth
- Daily ritual
- Quiet richness
- Rooted in nature
- Made with intention
- Skin, light, texture, and ritual

Avoid generic beauty language such as:

- Premium
- High-end
- Must-have
- Pamper yourself
- Game changer
- Obsessed
- Ultimate luxury
- Transform your life

## Editorial Imagery With Text

If an uploaded image already contains text, do not duplicate the same text directly beside it.

Place those images in moments where they can stand alone.

Ensure text within images remains readable on desktop and mobile.

If readability is poor on mobile, use a different crop or display the image in a contained format rather than full-bleed.

## Mobile Requirements

Mobile experience must feel intentional, not like a compressed desktop page.

For mobile:

- Crop images carefully
- Preserve faces, hands, products, and text
- Stack sections with breathing room
- Avoid tiny text inside images where possible
- Use contained cards for text-heavy graphics
- Keep CTAs visible and calm

## Final Review Checklist

Before finalizing, review the site and confirm:

- The imagery feels cohesive
- The site feels warm, not sterile
- The product feels integrated into daily ritual
- Scent cards and ingredient graphics are easy to understand
- Editorial images create emotional depth
- Mobile crops are beautiful and readable
- The website feels like luxury for living
- The overall experience reveals quiet richness rather than announcing luxury

The final website should feel like entering the kindest room in a beautiful home.
